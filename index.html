    Seven falls, then a spreadsheet.

    For most of my life I started habits and dropped them: a few weeks of running, a burst of DSA, then nothing. It hardly mattered until life got harder and the missing discipline started to show. So I'm starting again. Fall seven times, rise eight. This is the eighth, and that's the name.

    Since January the eighth try has lived in a Google Sheet called oze's Habit Tracker v2: thirteen tabs, a checkbox for every habit on every day, and a wall of COUNTIFs holding it together like sticky tape on a paper model. It worked. It also looked like a tax return.

    So I'm folding it into something lighter: the same habits and the same numbers, nothing to babysit, and a crane at the end of every good day. Every tick is a fold, and a missed day is only a fall. You get up, you fold again.

    These are oze's own numbers, from 1 January to 22 October 2026, the day before Eightfold was born.
